01   Core Features

WHAT WE BUILT

A full cinematic survival horror experience — no frameworks, no shortcuts.

🎚
7 UNIQUE LEVELS
Each level has distinct mechanics — puzzle-solving, corridor escapes, 1v1 combat, boss fights, and PvP. Every level has its own environment, AI, and game loop.
🧛
10 SURVIVOR AVATARS
10 unique 3D-rendered character avatars with distinct colors, names, and university majors. Fully selectable with confirmation flow before game start.
🤖
AI NPC MOVEMENT
NPCs use a real-time waypoint AI system with patrol logic, pause timers, and sinusoidal walk-bob animations — all running at 60fps in requestAnimationFrame.
💊
WEB AUDIO ENGINE
Custom Web Audio API synthesizer plays cinematic drones, impact slams, chimes, and horror stingers — all procedurally generated with oscillators and gain envelopes.
🏆
CINEMATIC FINALE
Level 7 ends with a full cinematic sequence: key glow → CSS chest unlock → treasure explosion → dramatic fade → glitch horror text → "THE APOCALYPSE HAS ONLY JUST BEGUN."
📷
ZERO DEPENDENCIES
100% Vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No frameworks, no bundlers, no npm at runtime. Loads instantly on any browser. Works offline as a PWA.
02   Game Levels

7 DEADLY TRIALS

Each level is a self-contained experience with unique mechanics.

1
THE LOCKED GATE
GMN College entrance. Find 3 hidden clues to crack the occult combination lock 13-7-3. Ambient red eyes glow from the corridor. Atmospheric horror puzzle.
Puzzle   Horror
2
THE FIGHT ARENA
10-man battle royale. Punch, kick, dropkick against 9 AI opponents with health bars, knockback physics, and guard system. Last survivor advances.
Combat
3
THE HAUNTED CORRIDOR
Dual-corridor maze. Stella AI hunts the player using chase logic, state machine (PATROL → CHASE → FLEE), and speed escalation. Squad NPCs Ben & Esha patrol independently.
Horror   Puzzle
4
THE SIX BOXES
Search 6 boxes for the Ancient Dagger. Activate it on the ritual altar. Stella boss appears with HP bar, chase AI, and disintegration death animation. Dagger lights up in hand.
Boss Fight   Combat
5
THE JUDGEMENT
Angel vs Stella. Moral choice system. Navigate the trial to decide Stella's fate. Full cinematic angel artwork with particle halos and divine light effects.
Boss   Horror
6
THE TRAITOR
Stella reborn. Electric corridor traps. Largest level with 96KB of game logic. Multiple game mechanics layered together. Current electricity traps as environmental hazards.
Final Boss   Horror
7
THE FINAL BATTLE
1v1 PvP arena. Player vs rival survivor. Punch, kick, dropkick combat with phase shifts. Winner gets the glowing key, unlocks the CSS-rendered treasure chest, and triggers the 4-phase cinematic ending.
PvP Final   Combat
