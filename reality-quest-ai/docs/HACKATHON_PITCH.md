# Reality Quest AI — 24-Hour Hackathon Pitch & Demo Guide

> **Theme**: REAL WORLD × AI × GAMING – Turn the Real World into a Game  
> **Product**: Reality Quest AI  
> **Tagline**: *Your camera is your weapon. Your city is your dungeon.*

---

## 1. Hackathon Pitch Deck (Slide-by-Slide)

### Slide 1: The Hook & The Problem
- **The Hook**: Millions of gamers spend 4+ hours staring at static virtual worlds inside screens while physical exploration is disconnected from play.
- **The Problem**: Location-based games (like Pokémon GO) rely on static pre-seeded stops (Pokéstops) and 2D map taps. They don't actually "see" or understand what's in front of you. A tree is just background noise; a water bottle is invisible.
- **The Question**: What if modern Multimodal AI could look through your camera lens, understand any physical object in real time, and transmute reality itself into playable RPG content?

### Slide 2: The Solution — Reality Quest AI
- **Everyday Matter Becomes Game Relics**:
  - Point at a **Tree** ➔ Synthesizes **Magical Sylvan Wood** (Crafting Resource)
  - Point at a **Water Bottle** ➔ Transmutes into a **Starlight Health Potion**
  - Point at an **Office Chair** ➔ Forges an **Aegis Kinetic Shield**
  - Point at a **Laptop** ➔ Siphons silicon logic into a **Quantum Cyber Blade**
  - Point at your **Dog** ➔ Manifests a **Solar Companion Familiar**
- **Dynamic AI Procedural Missions**:
  - The game reads your GPS coordinates and weather to generate living quests ("Ley-Harvest in the park", "Silicon Breach in the tech corridor").

### Slide 3: Live Demo (2-Minute Script)
- *See Section 2 below for step-by-step presentation script.*

### Slide 4: System & AI Architecture
- **Frontend**: React Native + Expo (Mobile iOS/Android) & Zero-Dependency Web Prototype
- **Vision Engine**: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision Multimodal Model (Sub-second classification, prompt-engineered RPG transmutation)
- **Mapping**: Geolocation GPS + Custom Cyberpunk Vector Map & Dynamic Radial Node Spawns
- **Backend**: Firebase Firestore real-time NoSQL sync, Cloud Functions, and Auth

### Slide 5: Market Potential & Monetization
- **Total Addressable Market**: $250B+ Global Gaming Market & $15B Location-Based AR Gaming sector.
- **Monetization Channels**:
  - Sponsored Real-World Scans: Retail brands can sponsor quests (e.g. "Scan any reusable water bottle for exclusive Cyber Armor").
  - Season Battle Passes & Cosmetic Holographic Card Shaders.
  - Energy & Inventory Expansion Packs.

- [x] **Theme Relevance (10/10)**: Directly merges the physical world (Camera + GPS) with Multimodal AI and RPG game mechanics.
